Purpose & Strategy

Defines why the company exists and the values behind its decisions, then translates that direction into objectives and a plan, with attention to continuity and adaptability.

Purpose & Values

Purpose explains the value the company seeks to create; values define the conduct expected when decisions are made.

Practical output: Purpose and values statement

Example: Before approving a growth opportunity, the board checks its alignment with the company's purpose and values.

Strategy

Translates direction into objectives, priorities, resources, responsibilities and performance measures.

Practical output: Approved objectives and plan

Example: An expansion plan identifies target branches, budget, accountable owners and performance review dates.

Sustainability & Resilience

Considers long-term value and the ability to continue operating and adapt to change.

Practical output: Long-term value and business resilience

Example: The company identifies alternatives to a critical supplier and prepares a continuity plan for supply disruptions.

Ownership & Stakeholders

Organizes relationships with shareholders and those affected by the company, protecting rights, participation and fair treatment.

Shareholder Rights

Organizes ownership rights, including information, participation and financial rights under the applicable framework.

Practical output: Fair treatment and protection of rights

Example: Shareholders receive the information needed for decisions under consistent procedures and criteria.

General Meetings & Voting

Organizes shareholder participation in matters within their authority and documents notices, voting and results.

Practical output: Participation and documented decisions

Example: The agenda and supporting documents are circulated, and votes and results for each item are recorded in the minutes.

Stakeholders

Identifies those affected by the company and establishes communication, feedback and grievance channels.

Practical output: Dialogue and grievance channels

Example: The company provides a supplier complaints channel, assigns a responsible owner and sets a response period.

Board & Committees

Builds the board's ability to direct and oversee through appropriate composition, clear mandates, productive meetings and ongoing evaluation.

Composition & Independence

Considers a mix of skills and independent judgment, with disclosure of relationships that may affect that judgment.

Practical output: Skills matrix and independence declarations

Example: The board reviews its skills matrix and updates independence declarations when circumstances change.

Committee Mandates

Defines each committee's duties, authority, relationship with the board and reporting arrangements.

Practical output: Approved committee charters

Example: The board approves an audit committee charter covering duties, membership, meetings and reporting.

Meetings & Decisions

Organizes meeting preparation, information, documentation of discussions and decisions, and follow-up.

Practical output: Minutes and action tracking

Example: Each decision records an owner and target date; progress is reviewed at the next meeting.

Evaluation & Improvement

Reviews board and committee performance and the quality of information, discussion and follow-up, then identifies improvements.

Practical output: Board and committee evaluation

Example: An evaluation identifies late meeting papers; the board sets an earlier circulation deadline and monitors compliance.

Executive Governance

Connects direction to execution through clear roles, delegated authority, accountability, incentives and leadership continuity.

Roles & Responsibilities

Clarifies the responsibilities of owners, the board and executives, including matters requiring review or approval.

Practical output: Separation of direction and execution

Example: The board approves direction and budget; management executes within its authority and reports performance.

Delegation & Accountability

Connects authority to limits, responsibilities, escalation and reporting, while preserving accountability for each role.

Practical output: Delegation of authority matrix

Example: A purchasing matrix defines who requests, reviews and approves transactions and the monetary limits at each level.

Incentives & Succession

Links remuneration to performance, conduct and sustainability and prepares replacements for leadership roles.

Practical output: Remuneration policy and succession plan

Example: The company approves CEO performance measures, names an interim replacement and develops potential successors.

Integrity & Conduct

Protects decision integrity through structured management of personal interests, related-party transactions, professional conduct and reporting.

Conflicts of Interest

Addresses situations where personal interests or relationships could affect objective decisions.

Practical output: Disclosure and recusal

Example: A director discloses a relationship with a supplier and abstains from the decision under the applicable procedure.

Related Parties

Subjects related-party transactions to appropriate review, approval and transparency of terms.

Practical output: Review and approval

Example: A proposed property lease from a related party is compared with market alternatives before submission to the authorized approver.

Conduct & Speak-up

Defines acceptable conduct and provides a reporting channel that protects confidentiality and supports fair handling.

Practical output: Code of conduct and safe reporting channel

Example: An employee can confidentially report suspected manipulation, with the investigation separated from the person concerned.

Risk, Compliance & Control

Connects risk to decisions, identifies applicable obligations and establishes controls that can be monitored and tested.

Risk Governance

Defines risk oversight and management responsibilities, acceptable risk levels, treatment and escalation.

Practical output: Risk appetite and treatment plans

Example: The company sets credit limits and procedures for breaches, and reviews them periodically.

Compliance

Identifies applicable legal and contractual requirements and assigns responsibility for monitoring and evidence of fulfillment.

Practical output: Obligations register

Example: The register links each license to its renewal date, responsible owner and evidence of renewal.

Internal Control

Establishes procedures to reduce errors and misconduct and tests whether they work in practice.

Practical output: Controls and effectiveness testing

Example: Supplier creation is separated from payment approval, and samples are tested to verify that separation.

Audit & Independent Assurance

Provides independent evaluation of governance and controls, linking audit findings to corrective actions whose completion is verified.

Internal Audit

Independently and objectively evaluates governance, risk management and control and reports to the appropriate oversight body.

Practical output: Independent assurance

Example: Internal audit reviews purchasing under a risk-based plan and reports findings to the audit committee.

External Audit

The external auditor expresses an independent professional opinion on financial statements within the engagement scope and applicable standards.

Practical output: Auditor's report

Example: The board or responsible committee discusses the auditor's report, key findings and matters requiring follow-up.

Addressing Findings

Turns audit findings into corrective actions, owners and dates, with effectiveness verified before closure.

Practical output: Correction and verification

Example: Management corrects a system access weakness; audit verifies the change before closing the finding.

Disclosure & Information

Provides reliable, timely information while managing data quality, access and protection.

Reporting & Disclosure

Defines financial and non-financial information, publication timing and responsibilities for review and approval.

Practical output: Financial and non-financial information

Example: A reporting calendar identifies required disclosures, reviewers and issue dates.

Data Quality

Supports accurate, complete, consistent, current data whose source can be traced.

Practical output: Reliable data

Example: Sales figures in the operating system are reconciled with financial reporting before the board receives performance information.

Information Protection

Controls access, sharing and retention in line with information sensitivity and protection requirements.

Practical output: Access rights and protection

Example: Board documents are restricted to members and authorized users, with access reviewed when roles or membership change.
